# 0.02 development acceptance audit

The eight implementation work packages are complete for the isolated-development scope of the original 0.02 plan. This scope is published as the v0.02 development prerelease. This is not a public-custody approval or an assertion that all proof-dependent cases have passed. Synthetic settled roots, guest execution, script verification and canonical CKB transactions remain distinct evidence classes. No CI was run. The separate native RISC-V experiment is prepared only after this acceptance and must remain unexecuted.

- **P**: `crates/tactus-o1-protocol/src/v02/tests.rs` and independent wire vectors.
- **E**: `crates/tactus-o1-execution/tests/v02.rs`.

## Final implementation review

WP1–WP7 are covered by the matrix above, the [resource review](V0_02_RESOURCE_REVIEW.md) and the pinned evidence records. Review of stale-input and quota reuse follows the actual authority chain: typed consumed Anchor/Vault, current exact Control dependency, exact immutable source records, and CKB live-cell enforcement. Including an irrelevant legacy dependency does not confer authority; replacing a required new role with it fails.

WP8 now exposes `Received` and `Soft` as **unobserved** by canonical recovery, per-batch `Ordered`/`Settled` observations, `Withdrawable` only on authorized claim exports, and actual `Paid` claim events. Ordering, settlement and payout each carry their own CKB inclusion height and confirmation count. An unsigned escape signing request is not marked Withdrawable. No safe confirmation threshold or permanent PoW finality is invented. Per-slot deterministic replay outcomes remain separate from API/recovery errors and from custody authority. Reports retain at most 65,536 compact outcomes; exceeding a retention limit aborts the refresh without deleting rights.

The latest canonical run is `artifacts/v02-canonical-abyeUlVB`. It checks Ordered batches with no settled state, authorized refund exports, two Paid refunds with distinct confirmation depths, and an actual P2P reorg removing the old branch's payment/ordering observations before new progress. The fresh-process node-outage check is `artifacts/v02-recovery-tools-lx9hunqh`. The synthetic lifecycle test separately checks different order/settlement/payment heights, reorg rollback and inconsistent coverage rejection; it does not prove a real SETTLE. Current covered suites total **136 passing Rust tests** (99 root, 19 adapter, 5 journal, 13 recovery). The final added legacy-genesis pair passes in the same adapter test function. [Final evidence](test-vectors/v02-acceptance-validation.json) fingerprints the closing sources and artifacts.

## Explicit next milestones

- **0.03:** real new proofs and two consecutive settlements, canonical asset release, proof/freeze and overlapping-settlement competition, and rollback of actual settled/payout state. These were assigned to 0.03 by the original plan; no execute result or injected root counts as completion.
- **0.04 and later:** authenticated incremental execution, persistent proving jobs, independent takeover at scale, and long-history resource measurements.
- **Public-product gates:** G2 admission fairness, appropriate confirmation/stall parameters, prover economics, contract-asset emergency exits, archive availability and whole-process resource qualification remain OPEN. This acceptance authorizes none of those claims.
